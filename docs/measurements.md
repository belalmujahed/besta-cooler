# Measurements needed before finalising

Calipers where noted, tape measure otherwise. Measure each bay; Bestå panels vary between frame types.

## Floor panel (drives the cassette: `T`, `cut`)
1. **Floor panel thickness** of each bay floor (calipers, at the front edge). → `T`
2. **Core type:** drill a Ø3 mm test hole where a cutout will go. Is it solid chipboard or hollow honeycomb? Also measure the top/bottom skin thickness.
3. Location and size of any **cam fittings, dowels, shelf-pin holes or rails** on the underside and top of each floor.
4. **Underside clearance:** distance from the panel underside to the bottom edge of the unit's front rail/plinth (how deep the scoop + baffle can go and stay hidden). → `scoop_t`, baffle height

## Bays (drives hole placement)
5. Internal width, depth (back panel → inside of the closed door) and height of each bay.
6. Back-panel offset from the wall, and whether the back panel stops short of the floor (a gap there is a free exhaust path).
7. Door bottom edge to the underside of the unit (existing shadow gap).

## Fan / hardware (after parts arrive)
8. Fan: confirm 120.0 × 120.0 × 15.0 and the 105 mm hole spacing; note which corner the cable leaves from. → cable slot position
9. Heat-set insert OD/length. → `insert_d`, `insert_depth`
10. **Actual cutout size** after cutting (each hole), and the corner radius left by the router bit/jigsaw. → `cut`, `cut_r`

## Electrical
11. PSU barrel: 2.1 or 2.5 mm pin.
12. Backlight strip run length (→ current: 12 V white is ~0.5–1 A/m) and where its feed enters.
13. Console USB port positions (for VBUS sense leads); confirm they power off in standby.
